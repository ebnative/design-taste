## Sourced context
MoMA connects De Stijl with the publication and art/design milieu of 1917; its approach extends beyond painting into other design disciplines.

## Visual and production principles
Planar relationships, horizontal and vertical organization, asymmetrical balance and limited color families.

## Our contemporary design proposal
Design a cover and carousel system with rectangular modules that accommodate changing headline lengths.

## Suggested exercise
Solve the same message in six asymmetrical layouts; check whether hierarchy survives when colors are removed.

## Limits and common misinterpretations
Do not copy the composition of a familiar work or mistake a color recipe for the movement as a whole.
