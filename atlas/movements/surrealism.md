## Sourced context
Tate explains Surrealism through rational experience, the unconscious, dreams and the pursuit of liberation; it is an international, plural movement.

## Visual and production principles
Unexpected relationships, the uncanny and varied methods such as automatic drawing and writing.

## Limits and common misinterpretations
