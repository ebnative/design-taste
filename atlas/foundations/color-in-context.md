## Sourced context
The Albers Foundation documents a teaching approach that examines color through relationships and experimentation.

## Visual and production principles
The same color can appear different in different surroundings; use comparative experiments.

## Our contemporary design proposal
Do not finish brand color selection with one HEX value; compare it on light and dark backgrounds and photographs.

## Suggested exercise
Place the same inner color in two different surroundings; note how perception changes while its measured value stays constant.

## Limits and common misinterpretations
Do not confuse this perceptual exercise with a file color-profile error or treat every color meaning as a universal rule of psychology.
