## Original contemporary method
Define actual small-size and one-color use conditions before evaluating mark/wordmark options. Compare optical spacing, reverse/background variants and varying content. Identification, typography, spacing and rights have separate gates; aesthetics cannot cancel failed required uses. Not every brief needs a symbol or monochrome variant.
