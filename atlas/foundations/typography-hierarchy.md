These are not universal prescriptions for typography.

## Original contemporary method
Compare size, spacing, line breaks, reading order and actual glyph bounds. Render at the final and smallest brief-required sizes. Check optical margins, kerning, leading, punctuation and font substitution. Nominal font sizes are not measured readability. Do not require a European-modernist style or Latin-only script treatment.
