# One brand idea, six platform narratives

Use this guide for social campaign concepts, platform adaptations and the relationship between branding, storytelling and visual craft. It is an authored method supported by scoped source observations, not a universal engagement formula.

## 1. Establish the brand truth

Read the existing brief and decisions. Write one sentence connecting an audience need, the brand's role and an observable act. Separate supplied facts, creative assumptions and measured outcomes. Do not invent founder history, product benefits, local availability, prices, endorsements or customer results.
Coordinate with brand-storytelling-studio for the brand narrative and design-process-storytelling for the designer's reasoning when those skills are available. Keep one narrative owner; do not duplicate interviews or manufacture a missing biography.

A bakery example: sharing becomes visible when one person offers another a piece of bread. This is a creative concept, not a historical claim.

## 2. Define a campaign contract

Record these invariants: core meaning, recognizable subject/product, approved identity assets, palette roles, type character, claim boundaries and tone.
Record these variables: use moment, narrative perspective, image, crop, pacing, information density, headline and requested action.
Consistency means recognizable meaning and identity across different scenes; it does not require the same source image or the same amount of text everywhere.

Generate materially different directions before a dependent aesthetic choice: a human gesture, a documentary ritual, or a conceptual still life, for example. Follow the user's existing checkpoints. Show real comparable sketches; preserve a provisional recommendation as provisional. Do not finalize dependent identity decisions while a selection is pending.

## 3. Assign a communication job to each surface

These are task-specific hypotheses, not demographic facts or algorithm rules. Change them when the actual audience and placement require it.

| Surface | Possible job | Bakery narrative | Useful next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram organic feed | Make one moment recognizable | An offered piece of bread | Share or respond |
| Facebook organic feed | Invite a community conversation | Add a place at a shared table | Tell a relevant story |
| TikTok | Show change through time | The last piece becomes two | Watch the resolution |
| Google advertising | Answer intent and clarify the offer | Bread for the next meal | Visit the relevant destination |
| LinkedIn | Explain a relevant professional ritual | Meet at the table before work | Reflect or discuss |
| YouTube | Promise a story worth watching | Follow bread from counter to different tables | Watch the actual story |

Specify organic/ad/profile/API and exact placement before selecting a format. Avoid forcing all six platforms into every campaign. A video keyframe or thumbnail is not a delivered video. Do not invent a business service solely to make a brand fit LinkedIn.

For each narrative, write: audience situation → communication job → scene/sequence → headline → supporting evidence → action → what remains constant → what changes.

## 4. Make the story visible

The proposed meaning must be supported by visible relationships: a reaching hand, an added plate, a sequence, a shared object. Avoid a justification that names symbolism absent from the image.
Connect material, palette, type scale and spacing to this specific role. Do not equate a color with a universal emotion or a serif with prestige.
Choose the headline from the scene and job. Keep an intentional hierarchy among headline, support and action. A carousel needs a sequence with a useful development, not a pile of unrelated cards.

Generate visual alternatives by changing narrative mechanism, not just palette or font. Reusing a source can protect identity; stretching it to every ratio can destroy composition. A new angle or scene must preserve product/person facts.

## 5. Separate Google asset families

- Uploaded Display is a completed fixed-format advertisement.
- Responsive Display combines separately supplied images, logos and text.
- Search image assets complement text ads; a decorated social poster is unsuitable.
- Demand Gen and Performance Max have their own placement, quantity and logo requirements.
- Merchant product imagery represents the item sold and belongs to a product data system.

Consult the current official product page before production. Do not carry a rule for Search into every Google product or infer a rule from equal pixel dimensions.

## 6. Research appreciated work without inventing performance evidence

Use public projects, dated counts and actual inspected images. Record the query, filters, visible sort label, loaded sample boundary, project/author, publication date, observation date, project URL and image/module locator.
Behance appreciations, views and comments describe Behance project activity. They are not the original social post's engagement or the campaign's conversions. Account for unknown project age, exposure, following and selection bias before making comparisons.
A Most Appreciated label does not prove returned cards form a strict numeric leaderboard. If ordering is nonmonotonic, disclose it; do not silently relabel the list as a global top ranking.

Selected public works inspected on 2026-10-16:
- [Social Media 2020, Felipe Pierre](https://www.behance.net/gallery/107523805/Social-Media-2020): a six-post board combines condensed display type, monochrome photos and blue interface metaphors. Transfer the contrast and changing image/text relationships, not the artwork.
- [Social Media Trindade, Kaká Fernandes](https://www.behance.net/gallery/116785707/Social-Media-Trindade): three inspected posts retain a dark palette, botanical vocabulary and linework while changing composition. Long copy still needs actual mobile-scale review.
- [Food social media post design, Syed Haseeb](https://www.behance.net/gallery/196891569/social-media-post-design-food-social-media-post-design): a fictional study presents product-led feed, banner, carousel and story treatments with controlled dark/yellow alternation. It supplies no sales proof.
- [Beau Bakery digital presence](https://www.behance.net/gallery/130986855/Beau-Bakery-2021-Digital-Presence): inspected bread and food applications pair cut-out product photography with open space and varied typographic scale. Exact font identification and mobile readability were not established.

For each observation record: visible mechanism → communication role → possible original transfer → limitation/counterexample. A popular project does not establish that a mechanism caused its popularity.
Do not copy unknown-rights artwork, fonts, screenshots or client data into this shared package.

## 7. Use REA only for a relevant evidence question

When requested or relevant and available, use reverse-engineer-anything for a public page's actual browser behavior, rendered structure or evidence capture. It does not replace visual critique, source attribution or campaign measurement.
Installing REA instructions does not establish an MCP connection. Use actual advertised tools or documented CLI operations; preserve returned version, Evidence IDs, declared scenario and limitations.
If a browser returns an error or challenge, record that observation. Do not infer inaccessible page contents or bypass access controls. Another permitted retrieval route may support separate claims; do not attribute its results to REA.

## 8. Produce, inspect and deliver

For requested raster examples use the available image generation skill. Lock exact copy and required invariants in each prompt. Keep deterministic typography and editable sources where the deliverable requires them. Raster concept artwork is not a native editable identity.
Inspect every distinct output for subject accuracy, anatomy, accidental text, hierarchy, visible margins, clipping and reduced-size reading. Apply design-composition-check and the relevant production skill. Record actual pixel dimensions; a requested size or upscaled canvas is not native image detail.
For accessibility use [WCAG contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) and contextual [text alternatives](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/). Distinguish a graphic review from whole-platform conformance.
Keep technical fit, self-critique, human selection, platform acceptance and performance as separate evidence states.

Deliver the brand sentence, six narrative cards when needed, the invariant/variable contract, source-backed format record, actual artifacts, prompts and observed QA limitations.
Maintain volatile platform specifications outside this stable method with source URL, scope, accessed date, publication/effective date, required/recommended/minimum distinction and unresolved fields.
Evaluate a testable hypothesis with an appropriate metric and comparable audience/time/budget. Do not invent an uplift or call a concept successful because files exported.
