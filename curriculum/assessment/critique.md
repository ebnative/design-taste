# Design critique

Read the brief, audience and desired feeling first. Inspect the result and actual preview.
Observation → communication effect → importance → proposed change → recheck.
Assess brief/meaning, visual organization, historical/cultural context, editable production and process separately.
Personal taste is not objective error. Do not produce findings about an image you have not seen.
A score or search ranking does not prove design quality. Technical checks do not replace aesthetic judgment.
