## 08. Editorial design and covers
Grids, reading order, page styles and relationships between content and material.

### Prerequisites
[03](03.md), [04](04.md), [07](07.md): type, source scope and adaptable layouts.
### Objective
Carry editorial content through brief, distinct concepts, provisional choice and checked publication layouts.
### Deliverable
Eight-page digital publication with declared page dimensions, three cover directions, selected editable cover and full/reduced exports; use [original cover example](../../examples/learning-guide-cover/README.md), not a copied design.
### Observable criteria
Reading order/page styles consistent; exact copy present; required-size cover check; rejection rationale and rights inventory retained; no press-ready claim without actual bleed/profile/print tests.
### Critique and revision
Follow [cover workflow](../../workflows/cover-editorial.md), identify one page/cover risk, revise and compare before/after under the same conditions using [assessment](../assessment/critique.md).
### Retained evidence
Brief, concepts, publication/source files, exports and revision log; native proof/human acceptance unrun or pending unless actually demonstrated.

**Suggested deliverable:** An eight-page publication, cover and production check.
