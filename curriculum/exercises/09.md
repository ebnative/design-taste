## 09. Motion preproduction
Define the purpose of movement; develop storyboards, styleframes and animatics.

### Prerequisites
[01](01.md), [07](07.md): visible hierarchy and format adaptation.
### Objective
Explain why movement/holds convey a message and retain a static alternative.
### Deliverable
Six 1920x1080 original storyboard/styleframes plus 6-10 second animatic with declared frame rate, or an explicitly blocked animatic and editable timing sheet when tools unavailable.
### Observable criteria
Each shot has purpose/time; exact text and adequate declared holds; motion avoids flashing/discomfort risks; static sequence communicates core content. Tool absence does not become completed video.
### Critique and revision
Review actual frames/timing, change one hold/transition, compare before/after; if playback unavailable, abstain on temporal perception using [assessment](../assessment/critique.md).
### Retained evidence
Sources, frames, timing sheet, actual playback/export receipt or blocked state, revisions and rights list.

**Suggested deliverable:** A six-frame storyboard and short animatic.

**Sources:** [S18: Ringling Motion Design course catalogue](https://catalog.ringling.edu/motion-design); [S20: SCAD Motion Media Design BFA catalogue](https://catalog.scad.edu/school-animation-motion/motion-media-design/motion-media-design-bfa/)
