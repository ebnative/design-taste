## 12. Portfolio and critique
Research question, alternatives, choice rationale, revision and self-assessment.

### Prerequisites
[04](04.md) and at least one completed production module [05](05.md), [06](06.md), [08](08.md), [10](10.md) or [11](11.md), with actual submitted evidence. Blocked native gates stay labeled.
### Objective
Present a traceable case study that distinguishes authored work, observed results and acceptance.
### Deliverable
Markdown case study, source/support ledger, three retained routes when brief requires, selected editable source, actual flat required-size outputs and at least one before/after revision.
### Observable criteria
Each decision resolves to an artifact/criterion; actual revision and recheck present; source/rights, software/artifact/native/human gates separate. No fabricated audience response, degree equivalent or mastery from authorship.
### Critique and revision
Run [assessment](../assessment/critique.md), identify one weak evidence claim or design risk, amend case study or artifact and recheck. Explicit reviewer/human response only if actually supplied.
### Retained evidence
Original/revised files, failures/partial outputs, raw responses privately if authorized, sanitized manifest, known limitations and exact next acceptance gate.

**Suggested deliverable:** One complete case study with before/after comparisons and production evidence.

**Sources:** [S04: Yale Graphic Design MFA](https://art.yale.edu/program/graphic-design); [S05: CalArts Graphic Design BFA](https://calarts.edu/academics/programs-and-degrees/bfa-graphic-design); [S10: KABK Graphic Design programme description](https://www.kabk.nl/en/programmes/bachelor/graphic-design/programme-description)
