## 10. Motion identity
Typography, transitions, rhythm, holds and translating a visual system into time.

### Prerequisites
[06](06.md), [09](09.md): identity uses and motion preproduction.
### Objective
Translate declared identity constants into time without losing identification or required text.
### Deliverable
Approximately 10-second 1920x1080 motion source/export at declared frame rate, static alternative and first/mid/final frames. Native operation only with an authorized live route.
### Observable criteria
Constants/variants identifiable; glyphs/copy preserved; holds/transitions checked in actual playback; no flash-based essential message; export duration/dimensions verified. Static source alone is not motion proof.
### Critique and revision
Compare one timing/transition change in actual before/after playback, document tradeoff and recheck; unavailable playback/native tools remain blocked, per [assessment](../assessment/critique.md).
### Retained evidence
Brand rules, sources, frames, timing/export receipts, revision comparison and untested native/audience states.

**Suggested deliverable:** An approximately 10-second motion experiment for the same brand.
