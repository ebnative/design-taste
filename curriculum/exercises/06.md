## 06. Logos and visual identity
Treat a brand as more than a single mark; define constants and variants.

### Prerequisites
[02](02.md), [03](03.md), [04](04.md): color, typography and claim scope.
### Objective
Build an identity system that meets declared use conditions, not just a geometric mark.
### Deliverable
Three original concepts, selected provisional editable mark/wordmark, BRAND.md and exact-size flat specimens. Declare smallest use, one-color/reverse/background and long-content cases before testing; e.g. 32px and 256px screens when relevant.
### Observable criteria
Distinct concepts/rejections retained; optical kerning/counters documented; required specimens legible/not clipped; cultural/script and asset/font rights uncertainty explicit. Trademark screening is not legal clearance; human/native gates separate.
### Critique and revision
Follow [identity operations](../../workflows/identity.md); retain before/after optical correction and same-size rechecks. Do not invent an accepted identity outcome.
### Retained evidence
Brief, concepts, refinement views, rights inventory, use manifest, [critique](../assessment/critique.md), native blocked/unrun receipt and explicit human decision or pending.

**Suggested deliverable:** A logo family, one-color tests and a short identity guide.
