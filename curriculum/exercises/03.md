## 03. Typography
Letterforms, spacing, hierarchy and long/short text; test the characters required by the intended language.

### Prerequisites
[01](01.md): evidence-based hierarchy description.
### Objective
Preserve exact copy and glyph coverage while testing typographic reading order and optical spacing.
### Deliverable
Three original typography-only 1200x1600 posters in editable SVG plus 300x400 views; font/fallback journal.
### Observable criteria
Title/supporting copy transcribed exactly; no clipping/collision at either size; observed kerning/leading/margins recorded; font substitution and script uncertainty explicit. Required unfamiliar scripts need qualified review.
### Critique and revision
Hold content/layout fixed, revise one spacing or scale variable; compare before/after at both sizes using [assessment](../assessment/critique.md). Reader comprehension is untested unless actual responses retained.
### Retained evidence
Copy brief, font inventory without binaries, original/revised sources and exports, glyph/optical findings and recheck. See [typography record](../../atlas/foundations/typography-hierarchy.md).

**Suggested deliverable:** Three typography-only posters and a process journal.
