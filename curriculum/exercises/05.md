## 05. Image-making and photomontage
Meaning, material, photography, illustration, masks and source rights.

### Prerequisites
[01](01.md), [03](03.md): hierarchy and exact typography.
### Objective
Construct an editable original image relationship with a traceable asset inventory.
### Deliverable
1200x1600 cover master and full/300x400 views plus asset-rights list. Original vector shapes are allowed when photo rights are unknown; native layered file only if actually authorized/available.
### Observable criteria
Elements remain independently editable; exact copy and required-size hierarchy intact; edges/lighting assessed relative to declared collage or realism goal; asset ownership/rights separate from source citation. SVG does not prove native PSD layers.
### Critique and revision
Compare before/after of one mask/edge/scale change and inspect full/reduced outputs; classify defect versus expressive preference using [assessment](../assessment/critique.md).
### Retained evidence
Original/revised masters/views, asset inventory, editability method, actual checks and blocked native state. Current-wave Photoshop is blocked by user decision; no script workaround.

**Suggested deliverable:** An editable cover composition and source list.

**Sources:** [S05: CalArts Graphic Design BFA](https://calarts.edu/academics/programs-and-degrees/bfa-graphic-design); [S31: MoMA Dada: chance, collage, photomontage and assemblage](https://www.moma.org/collection/terms/dada/chance-creations-collage-photomontage-and-assemblage)
