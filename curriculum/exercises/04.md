## 04. Reading through art and design history
Distinguish movements, schools and traditions; separate sourced context from contemporary proposals.

### Prerequisites
[01](01.md): separating description from interpretation.
### Objective
Resolve two historical claims to actual source passages without conflating a school, movement, technique or tradition.
### Deliverable
Two Markdown research cards, each with exact source ID/page/locator/access date/limits and an original contemporary proposal.
### Observable criteria
Correct record categories; quoted/paraphrased claim supported by actual inspected passage; imported versus independent review and text/image/font rights separate. Unsupported period/place or inaccessible source stays unverified.
### Critique and revision
Compare categories/context, identify one overgeneralization, revise claim scope and check source again. Do not promote an entire corpus or universal style ranking.
### Retained evidence
Before/after cards, short support passages, failure/uncertainty log and [critique](../assessment/critique.md); no third-party images copied.

**Suggested deliverable:** A research card comparing two approaches.
