## 11. Editing, sound and captions
Clip selection, narrative, audio relationships and readable captions.

### Prerequisites
[03](03.md), [09](09.md): typography and timing.
### Objective
Assemble a narrative with synchronized readable captions and controlled audio relationships.
### Deliverable
Approximately 30-second 1920x1080 edit with declared frame rate, editable project, video export, UTF-8 SRT captions and original/authorized media inventory. Missing tools mean blocked output, not synthesized execution evidence.
### Observable criteria
Caption text/time matches actual speech; no clipping in declared safe regions; overlaps/gaps and audio peaks reviewed during actual playback; every clip/sound right recorded. Proper names/diacritics preserved; unfamiliar language requires qualified review.
### Critique and revision
Compare before/after of one cut/caption/audio issue, playback same segment, retain actual recheck and [criterion classification](../assessment/critique.md).
### Retained evidence
Project/media provenance, exports/captions, timing/audio findings, revised segment and blocked/unverified production states.

**Suggested deliverable:** An approximately 30-second edit with project and output checks.
