## 07. Posters, flyers and social series
Preserve one message across different sizes and content lengths.

### Prerequisites
[03](03.md), [06](06.md): typographic/system constraints.
### Objective
Adapt hierarchy across formats without stretching or losing essential information.
### Deliverable
Original 1200x1600 poster, 600x800 flyer and four 1080x1080 social cards with editable sources and flat previews; include shortest/longest headline specimens.
### Observable criteria
Exact event/title/date/place copy; no collision/clipping; constants/variants named; essential text inspectable at declared display sizes, not just a mockup. Label situated communication choices rather than universal style superiority.
### Critique and revision
Compare long/short variants, revise one overflow/hierarchy risk and recheck all affected formats with [assessment](../assessment/critique.md).
### Retained evidence
Content fixture, before/after sources/previews, size manifest, actual rechecks and rights/font limitations.

**Suggested deliverable:** A poster, flyer and four-part social series.

**Sources:** [S07: UAL LCC Graphic and Media Design](https://www.arts.ac.uk/subjects/communication-and-graphic-design/undergraduate/ba-hons-graphic-and-media-design-lcc); [S40: Cooper Hewitt: How Posters Work](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/2015/02/05/cooper-hewitt-to-present-special-exhibition-how-posters-work/)
