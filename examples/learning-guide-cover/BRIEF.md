Message: learning is an active cycle, not innate authority or a promise of mastery.

Exact copy:
    Design Taste
    A field guide to seeing, making and revising
    Learning edition 01

Native PSD is not supplied or claimed. R3 live Photoshop is blocked by user decision.

All geometry/layouts are original task-authored assets. No third-party artwork or font files bundled. System Helvetica and Times are requested, with Arial/sans-serif and Times New Roman/serif fallbacks. Font presence is not activation or redistribution permission. Check recipient rendering after substitution; no license selected.
